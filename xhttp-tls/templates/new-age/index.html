Designed to stay out of your way

Most habit apps shout. Habitual whispers: it’s built around small steps, flexible schedules and honest feedback.

🌱

Start with a two-minute version of any habit and grow it at your own pace.

⏰

Smart reminders

Nudges that adapt to when you usually complete a habit — and go quiet when you’re on a roll.

Questions


Do I need an account?

No. Habitual works fully on your device. An account is only needed for optional sync.

What happens to my data if I cancel Plus?

Everything stays on your device and you can export it as CSV at any time.

Is there a web or desktop version?

Not yet. We’re working on a read-only web view for 2026.

How do I contact support?

Write to support@habitual.example — we usually reply within a day.
