Fog, forests and a castle you’ve already seen

It’s hard to write about a place that has been photographed ten million times. So I didn’t try to take the famous photo; I took a walk instead.

The path up the hill starts behind a small village and, early enough, you meet nobody but a woman walking her dog. Halfway up, the trees open and the valley appears, filled with fog.

By the time the tour buses arrived, I was already on a train to the next town, with a pocketful of pine needles and no regrets.

Clara Whitfield
