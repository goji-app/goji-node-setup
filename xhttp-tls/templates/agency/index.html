An independent studio since 2014

We started in a shared attic office with two desks and one printer. Today we’re four people in a former print shop near the Maas, still working on a handful of projects at a time so that every one gets proper attention.

Most of our clients come back: a bakery chain we rebranded in 2016 still works with us on seasonal packaging, and our first website client now has its third site with us.
