# Third-party photographs

The decoy templates contain photographs that were taken from the following MIT-licensed
open-source repositories (images recompressed and cropped; no other code or text was taken).
The photographs themselves originate from stock sites that allow free use (Unsplash/Pexels);
the repositories distribute them under the licenses reproduced below.

| Template | Photo source repositories |
|---|---|
| freelancer | paper-kit-2, soft-ui-design-system, material-kit |
| agency | startbootstrap-stylish-portfolio, startbootstrap-creative, soft-ui-design-system |
| creative | startbootstrap-creative |
| grayscale | startbootstrap-grayscale, material-kit, now-ui-kit |
| resume | soft-ui-design-system |
| blog | soft-ui-design-system, material-kit |
| new-age | soft-ui-design-system, material-kit |
| saas, analytics | soft-ui-design-system |

All people, companies, prices and texts on the sites are fictional. People shown in portrait photos are stock-photo
models and are not related to the invented names.
