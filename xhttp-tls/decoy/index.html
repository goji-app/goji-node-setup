Private beta

Product analytics without cookies, fingerprinting or third parties.

Quillmetric collects aggregate usage events from your apps and websites, keeps every byte on EU infrastructure and never builds individual profiles.

How we handle data

Cookie-free by design

Sessions are derived from short-lived, salted hashes that rotate every 24 hours. No consent banner required.

Tiny SDK

Under 3 KB for the web, native modules for iOS and Android. Events are batched and sent over a single connection.

EU-hosted

Ingestion and storage run on our own servers in the European Union. Nothing is shared with ad networks.

Funnels & retention

Answer product questions with funnels, cohorts and retention curves built on aggregated data.

Two lines to get started

Add the snippet, call track() where it matters. Events are queued locally and flushed to the telemetry endpoint in compact batches.

SDK access is available to beta participants.

import { init, track } from "@quillmetric/web";

init({ project: "your-project-key" });

track("checkout_completed", {
  plan: "team",
  seats: 5
});

Privacy

We do not store IP addresses, user agents or any device identifiers. Raw events are aggregated within 24 hours and deleted after 30 days. Quillmetric is currently invite-only while we stabilise the ingestion pipeline; we are not accepting new sign-ups at the moment.
